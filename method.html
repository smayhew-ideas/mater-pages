How the Source index is built

Companion to the Economic Torus and Figure 1, the Scissors. Draft, November 2026.

The Source band in the Economic Torus shows a measured index for 2015–2025. After 2025 the band follows one of two labeled scenarios, closed loop or open loop. This note records how the measured part is built, so anyone can check it, dispute it, or rebuild it.

1.0 is the pre-LLM baseline. A value below 1.0 means the human source is producing less in the open, or being diluted, compared with what its own pre-2022 behavior predicts.

Decisions and caveats

  • Q is trend-adjusted. Stack Overflow was already declining about 5.8% a year (2015–19, log-linear fit, 2020 lockdown spike excluded) before LLMs. Only the shortfall beyond that trend counts. Raw, it would show a 95% collapse and overstate the AI effect.
  • Q's 2025 figures come from a later update to the same compilation. Other published counts for late 2024 differ (one implies about 17,500 for December 2024 against 25,566 here), likely because of different query definitions. They should be pulled directly from the Data Explorer before publication.
  • C was measured to spring 2024. The 2023 value (0.95) is interpolated, and 2025 is carried forward. A separate 2025 study (Bouchaud and Ramaciotti, CNRS / Sciences Po) found about a quarter of the top 1,000 sites restricting AI crawlers, which is consistent with the carried-forward value.
  • H relies on AI detectors, which are imperfect, and counts AI-assisted writing as AI. It also plateaued at about 50% from 2025. That number doesn't flatter the thesis and is included deliberately.
  • W has a single reported year-over-year figure (2025). Earlier years are held at baseline, with no claim of decline.
  • Equal weights are a choice, not a finding. Changing them moves the 2025 index roughly between 0.45 and 0.65. The shape, flat to 2022 and then falling, holds under any weighting.

Not in the index yet

  • Cloudflare crawl-to-refer ratio (crawls per referred visitor, by AI company, on Cloudflare Radar). It is the most direct measure of extraction against return, but the series begins in 2025, so it can't anchor 2015–2025. It is the best candidate for a live gauge going forward. Disclosure: in Cloudflare's June 2025 snapshot, Anthropic had the highest ratio (about 70,900:1), and Cloudflare noted the figure is inflated by missing referrer data.
  • Trust surveys (Edelman, Pew) measure attitudes about AI, not the substrate itself.

The standing invitation

Every number above is third-party and public. The index claims nothing new; it only arranges what is already known. If it's wrong, the correction is a better number, and the table is built to take one.
