The Human Substrate · Figure 1

The Scissors: the draw rises, the Source falls

Text taken to train the largest documented model each year, against the health of the human source it came from. Both are indexed to 2020 = 1. The widening gap is the cost no ledger records.

Training data drawn (largest documented model) Source index (4 measured proxies)

Read it as a ratio, not a crossing. Both lines start at 1 in 2020 by construction, so where they meet means nothing. What matters is how far apart they move. By 2025 the largest documented model trained on about 120 times the text GPT-3 did in 2020, drawn from a source measured at just over half its pre-LLM health.

Log scale: equal vertical steps are equal ratios, so a halving and a doubling read as the same size.
